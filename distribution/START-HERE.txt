GTAV MOBILE — v0.1.0-alpha.3
Android + iOS, with automatic game setup from playgta5.
Experimental: builds are checked; real-device gameplay is not yet verified.

ANDROID
1. Open gtav-mobile-android.apk on your phone and allow installation when asked.
   Or connect one unlocked phone by USB and run Install-Android.cmd (Windows)
   or Install-Android.command (Mac). Enable USB debugging and trust the computer.
2. The USB helpers use Google's Platform-Tools. If missing, download them from
   https://developer.android.com/tools/releases/platform-tools
   and extract the platform-tools folder beside the helper.
3. Open GTAV Mobile, tap Download game, and keep the app open until setup ends.
   Tap Play. First launch compiles the game and may take several minutes.

Requires ARM64, Android 9+ and Vulkan 1.1. High-memory devices recommended;
minimum RAM and supported devices have not been established.
Mac terminal alternative: bash Install-Android.command

IPHONE / IPAD — MAC + USB
1. Install Xcode 26+ from the Mac App Store. Open it once and install iOS support.
2. Connect your unlocked iPhone by USB. Tap Trust and enable Developer Mode
   if prompted. Keep the entire extracted download folder together.
3. Open Install-iPhone.command, or open iOS-USB/GTAVMobile.xcodeproj directly.
4. In Xcode > Settings > Accounts, sign in with your Apple Account.
5. Select the GTAVMobile target > Signing & Capabilities. Enable automatic
   signing, choose your Personal Team, and replace "yourname" in the Bundle
   Identifier with your own unique name. Keep that identifier for future use.
6. Select your connected iPhone at the top, then Product > Run (Command-R).
   Xcode signs, installs and launches the game over USB. Trust the developer
   identity in iPhone Settings > General > VPN & Device Management if asked.
7. Open GTAV Mobile, tap Download game, and keep it open until setup finishes.

Free Personal Team signing lasts seven days. The app stays installed but stops
opening after expiry. Reconnect and Run again to renew it. Keep the same account,
team and bundle identifier; do not delete the app when refreshing its signature.
Save this extracted folder and your Xcode project for the next refresh.

The game executable is precompiled: no Rust or XcodeGen installation is needed.
An internet connection is needed for Apple's signing and the initial game data.
The Mac USB bundle includes the IPA and Xcode project; this iPhone USB flow
requires macOS and Xcode. Android also supports the Windows USB helper above.

iOS 15+; the original runtime recommends 8 GB RAM. Free signing does not grant
increased-memory entitlements. Device memory limits can affect startup.
Development-signed USB installation was verified on one iPhone. Gameplay and
weekly signature renewal have not yet been verified.
Apple's Personal Team details:
https://developer.apple.com/help/account/basics/about-your-developer-account

GAME SETUP
Use Wi-Fi. First setup downloads about 17 GB and installs about 16 GB.
Allow at least 19 GB free, plus extra space for Android's compiled game cache.
Both apps fetch pinned archives from alihammoud21/playgta5, verify SHA-256 hashes,
and save completed parts. If interrupted, tap Download game to resume; only the
unfinished part restarts. After setup, single-player data is available offline.
The desktop ZIP contains the apps and USB helpers; the game data downloads on
the phone during first setup. It is not a 17 GB offline installer.
Uninstalling the app removes its private game files and saves.
